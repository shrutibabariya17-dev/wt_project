About Student Hub

Student Hub is an integrated web-based student portal designed to provide university students with single-point access to course enrollment, campus events, academic performance records, club activities, and communication channels.

Project Mission & Vision

Our goal is to simplify university life by connecting academic departments, student clubs, and administrative services into one cohesive, accessible, and fast web application.
