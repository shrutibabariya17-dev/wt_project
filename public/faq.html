Frequently Asked Questions

Click on any question below to expand the answer:

What is HTML? +
HTML (HyperText Markup Language) is the standard markup language used to structure content on web pages.
What is CSS? +
CSS (Cascading Style Sheets) is used to style and layout web pages — for example, to alter the font, color, size, and spacing of your content.
What is JavaScript? +
JavaScript is a programming language that adds interactivity, animation, and dynamic data processing to web pages.
How do I enroll in semester courses? +
Navigate to the Course Enrollment page, enter your registered Student ID (e.g. 25aiml001), select your desired course from the list, and click "Confirm Enrollment".
Where can I view my academic performance and GPA? +
You can view your student profile, current semester SGPA, and attendance status on your personal Student Dashboard.
How do I register a new student account? +
Visit the Registration page, provide your Name, Student ID (format 25aiml001), Email, and Password. Upon submitting, your account is immediately saved in the database.
